AI animation has gone from “uncanny curiosity” to “actually usable in production” frighteningly fast. Here’s my current map of the landscape.

Video generation models

The big story is consistency. A year ago, characters would morph between frames. Today’s models hold character identity across shots, which is the difference between a demo and a tool.

The pipeline is shifting

Traditional animation pipelines are being reshaped:

StageAI role today
StoryboardingFully AI-assisted
KeyframesPartially automated
In-betweeningNearly solved
CompositingMostly manual

Manga-style generation

This is where things get wild. Panel-to-panel consistency for manga is still the hardest problem — keeping a character on-model across pages requires either strong reference systems or LoRA-trained custom characters. But the pace of improvement suggests we’re months, not years, away from fully AI-assisted serial manga.

What’s next

I’m most excited about real-time generation — animation that renders as fast as you can storyboard it. The gap between “idea” and “watchable output” is collapsing.
